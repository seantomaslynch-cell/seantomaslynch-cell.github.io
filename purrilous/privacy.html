Purrilous Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

This policy explains how Sean Lynch ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the mobile game Purrilous (the "Game").

1. Summary

  • Your progress stays on your phone. The Game has no account and no server of ours. Your save (cats, gear, coins, gems, settings) is stored only on your device.
  • We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location or health information.
  • The Game shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for a reward, and, after your first few runs, an occasional short ad between runs (at most 6 a day, never for Golden Ticket owners). iOS asks you whether the Game may track you across other companies' apps, and you can say no.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple and checked through RevenueCat.
  • Reminders are local notifications that your phone schedules for itself. We do not receive your notification settings.
  • We do not sell your information.

2. Information stored on your device

Your progress and settings, including chapters cleared, gear, talents, coins, gems, quests, the Season Pass, and your sound, music, vibration and reminder choices, are stored in the Game's storage on your device. We cannot see them. They stay until you erase them in Settings, delete the Game, or reset the device. Because we do not receive the save, we cannot restore it if it is lost.

The Game also keeps a small record on the device of which purchases it has already applied, so that erasing your progress and reopening the Game does not grant an old purchase a second time.

3. Advertising

The Game uses Google AdMob to show rewarded ads that you choose to watch in exchange for a bonus. To serve, measure and secure ads, Google may process your device's advertising identifier (if you allow tracking), IP address and the approximate location derived from it, information about how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data such as crash and performance information.

  • Tracking permission. On iOS the Game asks for permission to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. If you allow it, ads can be personalized using your advertising identifier. If you decline, ads are not personalized, and Google may still use limited identifiers for frequency capping, fraud prevention and aggregate reporting. You can change this any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent choices. In regions where the law requires it (such as the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), Google's User Messaging Platform asks for your consent before ads are requested. There, Settings shows a Privacy choices button that lets you reopen and change that choice.

Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. Watching a rewarded ad is always optional, and the Game plays without them.

RevenueCat does not receive your save. Its privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy. Purchase records are kept by Apple and RevenueCat as needed for tax, accounting and disputes.

5. Reminders

The Game can send reminders such as "your patrol is ready". It asks once, after your first finished run, and only if you say yes. The reminders are scheduled on your phone from your own save. They are never sent from a server, and no push token or notification data is sent to us. You can turn them off in the Game's Settings or in iPhone Settings → Notifications.

6. Device features

The Game uses sound, music and haptic feedback on your device. It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, precise location or Bluetooth.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Your save: kept on your device until you use Settings → Erase all progress or delete the Game.
  • Advertising and purchase data: held by Google, Apple and RevenueCat under their own policies. Erasing your progress does not cancel or refund anything you bought.

8. Children

The Game is a general audience game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port personal information and to object to certain processing. Because your save is stored only on your device, you can view and delete it there. For data held by Google, Apple or RevenueCat, use their own privacy tools or email us and we will pass on or act on the request within the time your law requires. California residents: we do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA.

10. Security

Your save is protected by your device's security and the iOS app sandbox. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure.

11. Changes

We will update this policy when the Game or its providers change, and revise the date above. If the Game ever starts sending your save or any personal information off your device, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
