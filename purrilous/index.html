Purrilous Support

Purrilous is a roguelike cat RPG. Your cat fights on its own, you pick its skills, and you build gear between runs. There is no account, and your progress is saved on your phone.

Need help? Email temveno@gmail.com. We aim to reply within a few days. It helps to include your iPhone model, your iOS version, the version shown at the bottom of the in-game Settings, and what happened.

Common questions

How does a run work?

Tap Start to begin a 30 day run in the chapter you are viewing. Each day is one battle that your cat fights by itself. Every level-up offers three skills and you keep one. Bosses wait on days 10, 20 and 30. Whatever coins and gear you collect are yours even if you lose.

My game is gone or I got a new phone.

Progress is stored only on the phone it was made on. There is no cloud save and no account, so it does not move to a new phone and we cannot restore it. Deleting the app deletes the save, and so does Settings → Erase all progress.

I bought something and it did not arrive.

Give it a minute, then open Settings and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account. Gems, packs and passes are one-time consumable items that arrive when you pay, so they cannot be restored later. The Golden Ticket is the one item that can be restored. If a payment went through and nothing was added, email us the date and the Apple receipt and we will sort it out. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What is the Golden Ticket?

A one-time purchase that gives you every ad reward without watching the ad. It is restored with Restore purchases.

Do I have to watch ads?

No. Ads only appear when you tap a button to watch one for a reward, and everything in the game can be played without them. There is no banner and no ad that interrupts a battle.

What are the chest odds?

Every chest shows its exact drop rates in the Shop before you open it. Tap Drop rates on the chest.

Can I turn the reminders off?

Yes. Open Settings and tap Reminders, or use iPhone Settings → Notifications → Purrilous. Reminders are scheduled on your phone. We do not run a notification server.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

If you are in a region where a consent choice applies (such as the EEA or UK), Settings shows a Privacy choices button. You can also change the tracking permission any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

The game is slow or a screen looks wrong.

Close the app fully and reopen it. If it keeps happening, email us with your iPhone model and iOS version.
