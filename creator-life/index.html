Preparing for iOS launch

The app is currently in development. This is its official support page. Store availability and final release details will be added when it launches.

Help with a purchase

In the iOS app, open the main menu → Settings & support → Restore purchases, or use Restore purchases at the bottom of the shop. Use the same Apple account that bought the pack and an internet connection. Content packs are permanent purchases, not subscriptions.

The Fame & Power Edition includes Creator Pro, Scandal & PR, Media Empire, and Fame Director. It is a separate purchase, not an automatic discounted upgrade from individual packs. Check the shop before buying if you already own packs.

If a purchase is pending, cancelled, or refunded, access follows the verified App Store transaction. For billing and refund requests, use Apple’s purchase support. Never email your password or full payment information.

Where are my lives saved?

The game has three local save slots and a Past Lives archive. They save on your device; there is no game account or cloud-save service. Restoring purchases does not restore a deleted life. Avoid removing the app or clearing its data if you want to keep your saves. Device backups may help, but recovery is not guaranteed.

Are ads required?

No. There are no forced ads. Sponsor rewards are optional: you choose whether to watch a rewarded ad. A reward is granted only when the ad provider confirms it was earned. If no ad is available or you decline privacy consent, keep playing and rest for free.

Report a bug or share feedback

Email your app version, iPhone/iPad model, iOS version, what happened, and steps to reproduce it. Screenshots are helpful. Please leave out personal or payment details. We do not have access to your local life saves.

Privacy questions

Read the privacy policy or email us. Ad privacy controls are available in Settings & support after the ad consent flow becomes available.
