Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This policy describes the current design of Creator Life: Fame & Power, developed by Sean Lynch. The app is being prepared for iOS release. We will update this page if the released app’s data practices change. Contact: temveno@gmail.com.

Your fictional life stays on your device

Character names, choices, relationships, story history, save slots, and settings are stored in local app storage. We do not operate a login system, social network, public player feed, or cloud-save server for this game. Its feed and people are fictional. We do not access your photo library, real contacts, microphone, or precise location. Character portraits are game assets.

Purchases

Apple processes payments. RevenueCat helps verify purchases, restore them, and determine which packs are owned. These services may process purchase records and transaction identifiers, an anonymous app-user identifier, device/platform details, and network information. We do not receive your card number. Purchase providers retain records according to their policies and legal obligations. See Apple’s privacy policy and RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Optional rewarded advertisements

The app requests a sponsor only when you choose to watch one. There are no banners, forced interstitials, or app-open ads. Google AdMob and its consent tools deliver optional rewards. Ads are requested as non-personalized; this does not mean no data is processed. The SDK may process IP addresses, coarse location inferred from an IP address, device/app identifiers and technical information, advertising interactions, diagnostics, and fraud-prevention signals. Availability and processing depend on your region, privacy choices, device permissions, and the provider’s configuration.

Where required, the consent form is shown before an ad request. You can revisit available ad privacy choices in the app’s Settings & support. Declining or withdrawing consent does not prevent you from playing the game. See Google’s privacy policy and Google’s advertising privacy information. We do not add an independent gameplay analytics or marketing-tracking service in this version.

When you contact us

We receive the email address, message, and attachments you choose to send to support. We use them to respond, investigate issues, and maintain support records. Send only what is needed. Our email service also processes messages under its own privacy terms. We keep support correspondence only as long as needed for support, dispute resolution, and legal obligations; you can request deletion.

Website hosting

These support pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process request and network logs when you visit. We do not add advertising cookies or analytics to these pages. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Your choices and rights

Deleting the app’s data removes local saves and settings, subject to any device backups you control. Store transactions and provider records are not deleted with the app. You can contact us about access, correction, deletion, objection, or other rights available under your local law; we may need to verify your request. For information held directly by Apple, Google, RevenueCat, or GitHub, their privacy channels may be necessary. Depending on the processing and your region, purchases are processed to provide the service you request, support uses our interest in responding to you, and advertising storage or access uses consent where required.

Children and international processing

This game is not directed to children under 13, and its creator-drama themes may not suit young children. We do not knowingly collect children’s information through support. If you believe a child sent us personal information, contact us. Third-party services may process data outside your country under their own safeguards. Final App Store age-rating answers will reflect the released content.

Security and changes

We limit the information we request and use established store and ad services, but no storage or transmission system is perfectly secure. Material changes will be described on this page with a new update date. For questions, email temveno@gmail.com.
