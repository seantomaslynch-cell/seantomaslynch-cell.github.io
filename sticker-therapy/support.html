Sticker Therapy Support

Sticker Therapy: Cozy Rooms is a calm room decorating app. Tap a + spot to add a sticker, hold a sticker to peel it off, and tap Done when your room feels right.

Common questions

How do I get my room packs back on a new iPhone or iPad?

Open Settings in the App and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with. You can also tap Restore purchases on any locked room.

What does "Try free" do?

Each locked room can be decorated once for free. The free try ends when you tap Done, and your finished room stays saved.

Where are my rooms saved?

Only on your device. Deleting the App deletes your rooms, so save the ones you love to Photos first.

A purchase did not unlock.

Tap Restore purchases first. If that does not help, email us with the date of the purchase and the pack name. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
