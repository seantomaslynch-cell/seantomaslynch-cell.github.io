Sticker Therapy Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy explains how Sean Lynch ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the app Sticker Therapy: Cozy Rooms (the "App"), on iPhone, iPad and the web.

1. Summary

  • Your rooms stay on your device. The App has no account and no server of ours. Your decorated rooms and settings are stored only on your device.
  • We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location or health information.
  • The App has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not use your advertising identifier.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple and checked through RevenueCat.
  • The App only saves to your photo library when you tap Save, and it never reads your photos.
  • We do not sell or share your information.

2. Information stored on your device

Your rooms, the stickers you placed, which room packs you have unlocked, free tries used, and your sound and haptics settings are stored in the App's storage on your device. We cannot see them. They stay until you delete them in the App, delete the App, or reset the device. On the web, they are stored in your browser's local storage for this site and are removed if you clear your browsing data.

3. Purchases

PurposeDataProvider

RevenueCat does not receive your rooms. Its privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy. Purchase records are kept by Apple and RevenueCat as needed for tax, accounting and disputes. The web version has no purchases.

4. Photos and sharing

When you tap Save on a finished room, the App asks for permission to add an image to your photo library. It only adds the picture of your room and never reads, uploads or deletes your photos. When you tap Share, the system share sheet opens and you choose where the picture goes. We do not receive it.

5. Device features

The App uses sound and haptic feedback. It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, location or Bluetooth, and it does not send notifications.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • Your rooms: kept on your device until you delete them in the App or delete the App.
  • Purchase data: held by Apple and RevenueCat under their own policies. Deleting the App does not cancel or refund anything you bought. Use Restore purchases to get your packs back on a new device.

7. Children

The App is made for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port personal information and to object to certain processing. Because your rooms are stored only on your device, you can view and delete them there. For data held by Apple or RevenueCat, use their own privacy tools or email us and we will pass on or act on the request within the time your law requires. California residents: we do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA.

9. Security

Your rooms are protected by your device's security and the iOS app sandbox. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure.

10. Changes

We will update this policy when the App or its providers change, and revise the date above. If the App ever starts sending your rooms or any personal information off your device, or adds ads or analytics, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

11. Contact

Sean Lynch, support@seanlynchgames.com. We aim to respond within 30 days.
