Privacy Policy

Endless Core: Mining Rush — last updated 27 August 2026

Endless Core: Mining Rush ("the app") is developed by Sean Lynch ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app collects, how it's used, and your choices.

No account required

The app does not require you to sign in or create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, or any other personal contact information.

Game progress

Your gold, upgrades, achievements, and other in-game progress are stored locally on your device only. We do not operate a server that receives or stores this data. On iOS, if you use the in-app Leaderboard, your score and achievement progress are sent to Apple's Game Center, governed by Apple's own privacy policy — we never see or store that data ourselves.

Advertising

The app is free to play and supported by ads served through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob (and Google, its parent company) may collect and process technical data such as your device's advertising identifier, general device information, and approximate location, in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's own data collection practices.

On iOS, the app asks for App Tracking Transparency permission before using your device's advertising identifier for personalized ads — you can decline this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and the app will still function normally with non-personalized ads instead. If you're in the EEA or UK, the app also shows a consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform) the first time it detects you may be covered by GDPR or UK data protection law, letting you choose what kind of ads you see.

On Android, ad personalization is based on your device's advertising ID, which you can reset or opt out of at any time in your device's Google Settings > Ads.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us using the details below and we'll remove it.

Your choices

  • Decline ad tracking at any time via your device's system settings (see the Advertising section above).
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored game progress from your device.
  • Contact us (below) with any question about your data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date at the top.
