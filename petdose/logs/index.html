Free printable pet medication logs

Simple one-page sheets you can print at home: a medication log, an insulin log, a seizure log, a vet visit checklist, and a pet sitter sheet. Every amount and time is left blank, because your vet decides those. No sign-up, no email, nothing to download but the page itself.

The printables

  • Free printable pet medication log
    Medication, amount from your vet, time, who gave it, and notes. Twenty rows on one page.
  • Free printable insulin log for diabetic pets
    For diabetic pets: date, time, insulin amount from your vet, glucose reading if asked, appetite, who gave it.
  • Free printable pet seizure log
    Date, start time, how long it lasted, what it looked like, what came before and after.
  • Free printable vet visit checklist
    What to bring, what to tell your vet, and questions to ask, with checkbox squares.
  • Free printable pet sitter sheet
    Who to call, feeding, medications with times, quirks, allergies and a do-not-feed list.

When paper is enough

A printed log is a good fit for a short course of one medication, when one person does the giving, or when you only need something to hand a sitter. It costs nothing, needs no battery, and your vet can read it at a glance. Keep it next to the medication, fill it in when you give the dose, and take it to your appointment.

When an app helps

Paper gets harder when there are several medications on different schedules, when more than one person shares the care, or when you want a long history without a stack of sheets. That is where an app can help. PetDose shows what is done, what is due and who gave it, warns you before a second dose, and sends a reminder only when something is overdue. Everything stays on your phone.
