Keep it on your phone with PetDose

Paper works well for a short course. If you would rather keep the log on your phone, PetDose is a free iPhone app for pet medications, meals and walks. It shows what is done, what is due and who gave it, warns you before a second dose, and sends a reminder only when something is overdue. Free to track, no account, and everything stays on your phone. Pro adds full history and a vet visit report you can send or print.
