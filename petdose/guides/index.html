Plain-language guides on keeping good records for your pet: what to write down, what to tell your vet, and what to do when a dose goes wrong. Each one links to a free printable you can use right away.

Keep it on your phone with PetDose

Want your records on your phone instead of paper? PetDose is a free iPhone app for pet medications, meals and walks. It shows what is done, what is due and who gave it, warns you before a second dose, and sends a reminder only when something is overdue. Free to track, no account, and everything stays on your phone. Pro adds full history and a vet visit report you can send or print.
