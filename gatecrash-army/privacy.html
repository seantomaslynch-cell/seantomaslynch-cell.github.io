Gatecrash Army Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Gatecrash Army ("the game") is made by Sean Lynch. This policy explains what information the game and the services built into it handle.

The short version

The game has no accounts and no server of its own. Your progress (level, coins, upgrades, skins and settings) is saved only on your device. Ads in the game are non-personalized, and the game never asks to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Information handled by third-party services

ServiceWhat it doesInformation it may process
Google AdMobShows ads, including optional reward videos. Every ad request is marked non-personalized.Device identifiers, IP address (used for approximate location and fraud prevention), ad interaction data, diagnostics.
RevenueCat, Apple App Store and Google PlayProcesses in-app purchases and restores them.Purchase history and an anonymous app user ID. Payment details are handled by Apple or Google, never by the game.
GameAnalyticsTells us how levels play (for example, which levels players fail most) so we can balance the game.An anonymous ID, device model and OS version, and in-game events such as levels started and completed.
Sentry (crash reports)If the game crashes or hits an error, a report helps us fix it.The error message and code location, device model and OS version, and the game version. No personal information.

None of this information is linked to your name or used to track you. These providers have their own privacy policies: Google, RevenueCat and GameAnalytics.

Consent in the EEA and UK

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the game shows Google's consent form before any ad loads, and your choice is respected.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game deletes your saved progress from the device. Because the game has no account, we hold no personal data to delete. To reset the advertising identifier, use your device's privacy settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
