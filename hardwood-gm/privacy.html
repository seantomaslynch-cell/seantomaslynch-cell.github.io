Privacy policy

Last updated 2 September 2026

Hardwood GM is a single-player basketball management game. This policy is short because the app does very little with information about you.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. There's no name, email, phone number, or account of any kind, and no sign-in. Your general manager, your team, and your league are written only to storage on your own device — we never receive any of it, and it's gone if you uninstall the app.

Advertising

Hardwood GM is free, supported by optional rewarded video adverts. You're never shown one you didn't choose to watch, and the game is fully playable without ever watching one.

Adverts are served by Google AdMob. To serve them, AdMob may collect an advertising identifier and technical information about your device (model, OS version, coarse region, and how you interact with an advert). We don't receive this information ourselves and can't link it to you.

  • Google's practices: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • iOS — you'll be asked for tracking permission before any ad loads. Decline and ads still work, just less personalised.
  • Android — Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID, any time.

Purchases

Hardwood GM offers one purchase: the Front Office Pass, a permanent unlock for extra start scenarios. It's sold through Apple's App Store and validated by RevenueCat, a purchase-management service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous device identifier and your purchase receipt — never your name, card number, or billing details, which Apple handles entirely on its own.

Children

Hardwood GM isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. Ads served to users Google identifies as children are configured as non‑personalised.

Analytics

None. No analytics SDK, no crash reporting service, no telemetry of any kind.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there's nothing for us to export or delete. To remove everything the app has stored, delete your career from the setup screen or uninstall the app.
