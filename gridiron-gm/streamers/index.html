Add it to your screen

The easiest way. Each file below is a transparent picture the size of your screen with the card already in the corner. Add it as one more layer over your game, and you are done. The link tags are already in the QR code.

  1. OBS Studio: Sources, add Image, pick a 1080p PNG. To use the animated loop, add Media Source instead, pick the WebM and tick Loop.
  2. Streamlabs, Twitch Studio, TikTok LIVE Studio: add an Image layer, pick the PNG, and stretch it to fill the whole canvas.
  3. Streaming from your phone: use a vertical file. In your streaming app, add an Image overlay and set it to full screen.

Promo overlay

A small card that slides in every minute or so with the game name, a one-line pitch and a QR code. Your handle goes into the link, so installs from your stream show up under your name.

1. Overlay for OBS

In OBS: Sources, add Browser, paste this as the URL, set width 1920 and height 1080. Nothing else to change.

2. Chat command

Paste as the reply for a !gridiron command in Nightbot, StreamElements or Streamlabs.

3. Your download link

Change how it behaves by adding options to the overlay link:

OptionWhat it doesDefault
pos=brCorner: bl, br, tl or trbl
interval=120Seconds between appearances75
show=10Seconds the card stays up12
badge=0Hide the small corner tag between cardson
scale=0.8Make everything smaller or larger1
demo=1Show cards right away, to test your layoutoff

A few ground rules

  1. Free to use on streams, videos and social posts about Gridiron GM. No permission needed, no fee.
  2. Please keep the logo unmodified apart from resizing, and leave the disclaimer on the scenes: the teams and players are made up and the game has no link to any pro football league.
  3. The game is free with optional rewarded ads and optional purchases. If your stream is sponsored, follow your platform's disclosure rules.
  4. The game is iPhone only for now.
