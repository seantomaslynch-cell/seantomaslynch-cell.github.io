PlateShot Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sean Lynch ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the mobile app PlateShot, listed on the App Store as "PlateShot — Macro Logger" (the "App").

1. Summary

  • Your meal log stays on your phone. PlateShot has no account and no PlateShot server. Meals, photos, macros, water, shot-day notes, favorites and targets are stored only on your device.
  • We do not ask for your name, email address or access to your health records.
  • Subscriptions are processed by Apple and checked through RevenueCat.
  • The App has no ads, no product analytics or crash-reporting service, and no tracking. We do not sell your information.
  • PlateShot is not a medical device. Calorie and macro numbers are estimates for your own information.

2. Information you enter

Meals, photos you attach to a meal, calorie and macro numbers, water, optional shot-day checkboxes and notes, favorites, and your daily targets. This information is kept in the App's storage on your device. We cannot see it. Shot-day checkboxes are optional reminders for you. They are not medical records and they stay on the device.

Your log leaves your phone only if you choose to export it. Export (a Pro feature) creates a JSON file that you send wherever you choose through the iOS share sheet.

3. Photo estimates

When you photograph a meal, the current version of the App produces the calorie and macro estimate on your device. The photo is not uploaded.

A future version may offer cloud-based estimates. If it does, the photo you choose would be sent to the AI provider (OpenAI) only to return a nutrition estimate for that photo, PlateShot would not keep a copy of it on any PlateShot server, and this policy will be updated before that version is released.

4. Camera and photo library

The App asks for camera access so you can photograph a meal, and for photo library access so you can pick an existing picture. Both are optional. If you decline, you can still log meals by typing them in.

RevenueCat does not receive your meal log or your photos. We do not receive your real-world identity, your card number or your billing address from Apple or RevenueCat. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

6. Subscriptions

PlateShot Pro is sold as an auto-renewing subscription (weekly, monthly or annual). Payment is charged to your Apple Account, and you manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple Account settings. Restore purchases is available on the paywall and in Settings.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Your meal log: kept on your device until you delete it. Open Settings → Delete all data to erase meals, favorites and goals on that device, or delete the App.
  • Purchase records: retained by Apple and RevenueCat as required for tax, accounting and disputes. Deleting your data in the App does not cancel a subscription. After a reinstall, use Restore purchases.

8. Children

The App is intended for adults managing their own nutrition and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal information and to object to processing. Because your log is stored only on your device, you can export or delete it directly in the App. For anything held by our providers, email us and we will pass on or act on the request within the time your law requires. California residents: we do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA.

10. Security

Your log is protected by your device's security and the iOS app sandbox. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure.

11. Changes

We will update this policy when the App changes and revise the date above. If the App ever starts sending your photos or your log off the device, this policy will be updated before that release.

12. Contact

Sean Lynch, temveno@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 30 days. Please do not send medical questions. PlateShot is not a medical device and cannot give clinical advice.
