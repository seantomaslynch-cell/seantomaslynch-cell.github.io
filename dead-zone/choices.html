Privacy Choices

Controls available in version 1.0

Privacy Options in the Game

Open Dead Zone’s main menu and select Privacy & Data to reopen Google’s available consent or privacy options. The options shown depend on your region and the messages configured in Google AdMob Privacy & messaging.

Advertising choices

Dead Zone requests non-personalized ads and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission. Rewarded ads are optional: declining or closing one simply means the advertised reward is not granted. You can report an inappropriate ad using the information control provided on the ad or by contacting us with the date, time, and a description or screenshot.

Apple controls

You can review system privacy permissions at any time in iOS Settings. Dead Zone does not request camera, microphone, contacts, photos, precise location, or Bluetooth access.

Delete local game progress

The Game’s save is stored on the device. Deleting Dead Zone removes its local save and settings. Version 1.0 has no Dead Zone account or cloud save to delete.

Contact us

Use the Dead Zone support form for non-sensitive questions. GitHub issues are public. Do not post passwords, government identifiers, payment information, precise location, or other personal information.
