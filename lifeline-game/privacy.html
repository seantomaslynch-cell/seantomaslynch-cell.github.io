LifeLine Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-21

Effective date: 2026-09-21

This Privacy Policy explains how Sean Lynch ("we", "us", the "Developer") handles information in connection with the mobile game LifeLine (the "Game"). If you do not agree with this policy, do not use the Game.

1. Summary

  • The Game is single-player. Your life-sim save data stays on your device. We do not operate accounts and we do not have a server that stores your game.
  • We show ads and offer optional in-app purchases. The companies that provide those services (Google AdMob and RevenueCat) process limited technical data to make them work and to prevent fraud.
  • On iPhone, TikTok's own measurement tool asks for tracking permission (the standard Apple prompt, shown a few seconds after first launch) so it can tell whether a TikTok ad led to your install and, if you buy something, which ad led to that purchase. Declining changes nothing else about the Game — ads inside LifeLine stay non-personalised either way, and this permission is unrelated to them.
  • We use an analytics tool (PostHog) to understand how the Game is used in aggregate. Usage analytics are off by default and only begin if you turn them on, either when the Game asks once on the main menu or in Settings > Privacy. While they are off, nothing about how you play leaves your device. While they are on, the events listed in Section 2.3 are sent to PostHog, keyed to a random install identifier and never to your name, your character's name or your story. Turning them off again stops collection and discards anything not yet sent.
  • We do not sell your personal information. We do not knowingly collect data from children under the age required by your local law.

2. Information processed

2.1 Data you provide

The Game does not ask for your name, email, address, phone number, photos, or contacts. Character names and choices you type exist only inside your local save file.

2.2 Data stored on your device

  • Your game save (progress, characters, settings, purchase-entitlement flags). Stored in the operating system's app storage and, on Apple devices, the Preferences store. You can export or permanently delete it from Settings → Your data inside the Game.

3. Legal bases (EEA / UK users)

  • Consent, for storing and reading information on your device for advertising, and optional analytics.
  • Legitimate interests, for non-personalised advertising and fraud prevention.
  • Contract, for processing an in-app purchase you request.

You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings → Privacy & ads or through your device's ad-settings and tracking controls.

4. Advertising and your choices

  • Ads are never personalised. Every ad request the Game makes is flagged non-personalised, regardless of your settings or region. We do not build an advertising profile of you and we do not sell or share your data.
  • Tracking permission, when you see it, is TikTok's alone, and only for attribution. On iPhone, TikTok's own measurement tool (Section 2.3) asks Apple's standard tracking-permission question a few seconds after first launch, so TikTok can tell whether one of its ads led to your install or purchase. It is not used to target ads at you inside the Game — ads here stay non-personalised regardless of your answer — and declining it does not change anything else about how the Game works.
  • In the EEA and UK you will see a consent prompt from Google's consent tool on first ad load. It covers storing and reading information on your device, which non-personalised ads still do. It does not cover personalisation, which we do not do either way.
  • Every ad is opt-in. The Game shows no banners and no interstitials, and never interrupts play with one. Ads appear only when you choose to watch one for a bonus, and the whole Game is playable without ever watching one.
  • Owning Plus or the Everything Pass gives you every ad bonus free, so you never need to watch an ad at all.

More about Google's ad data practices: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>

5. In-app purchases

Purchases are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play and by RevenueCat. We receive confirmation that a purchase or restoration succeeded and a non-identifying transaction reference. We never receive your card number or billing address. Refunds are handled by the store, not by us.

6. Data retention

  • On-device save: kept until you delete it or uninstall the Game.
  • Ad and anti-fraud data: retained by the ad provider per its own policy.
  • Purchase records: retained by the stores and RevenueCat as required for tax, accounting, and dispute resolution.
  • Usage analytics (only if you turned them on): kept by PostHog for the retention period of our plan and then deleted. The events carry a random install identifier and nothing that names you. The Game ships no crash reporting tool, so no crash data is collected.

7. Sharing

We share data only with the service providers listed in Section 2.3, acting on our instructions, and only as needed for the stated purpose. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights. We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising beyond the ad function described above (which you control via consent).

8. Children

The Game is rated for mature audiences and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly process personal data from anyone under the minimum age set by their local law (for example, 13 in the United States, 16 in parts of the EEA). If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

All advertising in the Game is non-personalised, for every user, in every region, including any user for whom age-appropriate treatment applies.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Because the Game stores your save locally, you can exercise deletion directly in Settings → Your data → Delete all data. For anything held by our providers, email temveno@gmail.com and we will pass the request on or action it within the time your law requires. Usage analytics are keyed to a random install identifier rather than to you, which means we cannot look your events up from a name or an email address. You can stop any further collection yourself at any time in Settings → Privacy, which also discards anything not yet sent.

California residents: we do not sell or "share" personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA, and we do not use sensitive personal information for inferring characteristics.

10. Security

We rely on the operating system's app sandbox and on our providers' security programmes. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

11. International transfers

Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, transfers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent safeguard offered by the provider.

12. Changes

We will update this policy as the Game changes and will revise the "Last updated" date. Material changes will be surfaced in the Game.
