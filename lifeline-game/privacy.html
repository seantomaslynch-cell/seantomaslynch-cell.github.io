LifeLine — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-30

Effective date: 2026-08-30

This Privacy Policy explains how Sean Lynch ("we", "us", the "Developer") handles information in connection with the mobile game LifeLine (the "Game"). If you do not agree with this policy, do not use the Game.

1. Summary

  • The Game is single-player. Your life-sim save data stays on your device. We do not operate accounts and we do not have a server that stores your game.
  • We show ads and offer optional in-app purchases. The companies that provide those services (Google AdMob and RevenueCat) process limited technical data to make them work and to prevent fraud.
  • We use a crash-reporting and analytics tool to understand stability and how the Game is used in aggregate. Usage analytics are off by default and only begin if you turn them on, or where local law treats them as permitted.
  • We do not sell your personal information. We do not knowingly collect data from children under the age required by your local law.

2. Information processed

2.1 Data you provide

The Game does not ask for your name, email, address, phone number, photos, or contacts. Character names and choices you type exist only inside your local save file.

2.2 Data stored on your device

  • Your game save (progress, characters, settings, purchase-entitlement flags). Stored in the operating system's app storage and, on Apple devices, the Preferences store. You can export or permanently delete it from Settings → Your data inside the Game.

3. Legal bases (EEA / UK users)

  • Consent — personalised advertising and optional analytics.
  • Legitimate interests — non-personalised advertising, fraud prevention, crash diagnostics necessary to keep the Game working.
  • Contract — processing an in-app purchase you request.

You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings → Privacy & ads or through your device's ad-settings and tracking controls.

4. Advertising and your choices

  • On first run (or first ad load) in regions that require it, you will see a consent prompt from Google's consent tool. Your choice controls whether ads are personalised.
  • On Apple devices, personalised advertising also requires you to allow tracking when iOS asks (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, ads still appear but are non-personalised.
  • You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking / Apple Advertising; Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Purchasing the Lifetime Pass removes all advertising.

More about Google's ad data practices: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>

5. In-app purchases

Purchases are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play and by RevenueCat. We receive confirmation that a purchase or restoration succeeded and a non-identifying transaction reference. We never receive your card number or billing address. Refunds are handled by the store, not by us.

6. Data retention

  • On-device save: kept until you delete it or uninstall the Game.
  • Ad and anti-fraud data: retained by the ad provider per its own policy.
  • Purchase records: retained by the stores and RevenueCat as required for tax, accounting, and dispute resolution.
  • Crash/analytics data: retained by the provider for a limited window (typically up to 90 days for crashes) then deleted or aggregated.

7. Sharing

We share data only with the service providers listed in Section 2.3, acting on our instructions, and only as needed for the stated purpose. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights. We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising beyond the ad function described above (which you control via consent).

8. Children

The Game is rated for mature audiences and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly process personal data from anyone under the minimum age set by their local law (for example, 13 in the United States, 16 in parts of the EEA). If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

Advertising shown to any user for whom age-appropriate treatment applies is non-personalised.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Because the Game stores your save locally, you can exercise deletion directly in Settings → Your data → Delete all data. For anything held by our providers, email temveno@gmail.com and we will pass the request on or action it within the time your law requires.

California residents: we do not sell or "share" personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA, and we do not use sensitive personal information for inferring characteristics.

10. Security

We rely on the operating system's app sandbox and on our providers' security programmes. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

11. International transfers

Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, transfers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent safeguard offered by the provider.

12. Changes

We will update this policy as the Game changes and will revise the "Last updated" date. Material changes will be surfaced in the Game.
